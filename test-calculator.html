📐 Calculation Steps

📚 Formula Explanation

The calculator uses the Standard Amortized Loan Formula:

M = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1] Where: M = Monthly Payment P = Principal (Loan Amount) r = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate ÷ 12) n = Number of Months
